# Roadmap

The roadmap is gated by evidence and maintenance capacity, not page-count targets.

## A Track: Formats, Learn, And Tools v1

**Target: weeks 1-6**

- Establish the charter, taxonomy, methodology, governance, and licensing.
- Implement structured records, validation, fixtures, and exports.
- Publish at least 10 format profiles and 5 learning guides.
- Publish at least 20 evidence-backed tool profiles.
- Generate and visually verify the public index experience.
- Invite represented maintainers to correct factual profiles.

Completion requires a clean-checkout build, source and license audit, accessibility review, transparent affiliation, and public correction workflow.

## B Track: Dataset Registry

**Target: months 2-3**

Activate only after provenance, licensing, access, version, removal, and permitted-use fields validate against real examples. Publish the navigation section after at least three credible dataset records exist.

## C Track: Benchmark Registry And Original Evaluation

**Target: months 3-6**

First catalog existing relevant evaluations without flattening incompatible results. Then publish one reproducible brownfield context evaluation with raw inputs, environment, failures, and outputs.

No overall leaderboard is planned.

## D Track: specs.place Corpora

**Target: month 6+**

Integrate only explicitly published, provenance-rich corpora. Consent, licensing, removal, versioning, and permitted-use controls must be operational before ingestion. DevSpecs and specs.place remain usable independently.

## E Track: Governance Review

**Target: months 6-12**

Consider a dedicated organization only after independent maintainers, contributors, citations, and research use exist. Repository transfer is an outcome of independent governance, not a substitute for it.
