## Review A Profile

If you maintain or use a represented tool:

1. Read its profile under [Tools](tools/README.md).
2. Check capabilities, deployment and storage, agent support, limitations, and sources.
3. [Submit a profile review](https://github.com/devspecs-com/agentic-development-index/issues/new?template=profile-review.yml), [correct a fact](https://github.com/devspecs-com/agentic-development-index/issues/new?template=factual-correction.yml), or [dispute an interpretation](https://github.com/devspecs-com/agentic-development-index/issues/new?template=disputed-claim.yml).

Confirmation is not an endorsement. No backlink, installation, or promotional statement is requested.
