# Asynchronous handoffs

## What You Will Learn

Create a compact handoff that lets the next human or agent review unattended work without reopening chat.

## Why It Matters

The diff shows physical changes but does not reliably preserve the goal, rejected alternatives, or next gate.

## Workflow

Record the attempted slice, files changed, checks run, decision, known misses, and recommended next action.

## Tradeoffs

Structured handoffs cost a small amount of time and should be reserved for work where continuity matters.
